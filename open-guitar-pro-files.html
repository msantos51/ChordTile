Got a .gp5, .gpx or .gp file and no Guitar Pro? Open it in your browser with ChordTile: hear the song, follow the tab as it plays, slow it down and loop the part you are learning. Nothing to install, on computer or phone.

How to open a Guitar Pro file in three steps

  1. Open the player and create a free account. It takes a minute and needs no card. (Without an account you can try the sample song.)
  2. Load the file. Press “Load a Guitar Pro file” and choose it, or drag it onto the page.
  3. Pick your instrument and press ▶. Choose your track (guitar, bass…) in the instrument menu. The tab scrolls with the sound, bar by bar.

The song is saved in your account, so it is there on every device you sign in to. It stays private: it is never shared with anyone.

You hear every track in the file, the whole band. You pick the one you play (guitar, bass, keys…) and the tab and chords follow it.

What you can do with the file

  • Play it and follow the tab. A cursor shows what is playing and the current bar lights up.
  • Slow it down to 25%, 50% or 75% (or speed it up to 125%) without changing the pitch.
  • Loop a section or a single bar until it sits under your fingers.
  • See the song as a map. Each bar is a tile showing its chord, coloured by note and grouped by section (Intro, Verse, Chorus…), so you see the shape of the song at a glance.
  • Play along or solo your part. Mute your instrument and play over the band, or hear only your track.
  • Count every bar. Open a bar large and see how to count it (“1 & 2 &”), with every technique explained: bends, slides, hammer-ons, palm mutes…
  • Export a PDF chart of the whole song on one A4 page, to print or take to rehearsal.
  • Learn it without the tab with Tile by Tile: one section with the tab, then without, then joined to the rest.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Guitar Pro to open a .gp5 or .gpx file?

No. ChordTile opens Guitar Pro files from version 3 to 8 in your browser, with nothing to install. You only need a free account to load your own files.

Is it free?

Yes. A free account opens your files and keeps up to 3 songs, with the tab, the chord chart, speed control and the metronome. Premium (€3.99 a month, €29 a year or €49 once) adds unlimited songs, loops, Tile by Tile and PDF export. New accounts get 7 days of Premium, no card needed.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It works in the browser on iPhone and Android, and you can add it to your home screen to open it like an app. See on your phone.

Can I edit the tab?

No. ChordTile is made for reading, listening and practising, not for writing tabs. You can rename and split sections and add notes to bars, but the notes of the tab stay as they are in the file. To edit them, use Guitar Pro, TuxGuitar or MuseScore Studio.

My file won’t open. What can I do?

Check that it is a Guitar Pro (3 to 8) or MusicXML file. If it opens in Guitar Pro but not here, save it as .gp5 there and try again. Still stuck? Contact us.
