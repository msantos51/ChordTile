How it works

From file to rehearsal in three steps.

  1. 1

    Load the tab

    Pick a Guitar Pro file of the song you want to play and choose your instrument.

  2. 2

    See the song map

    Intro, verse, chorus: each section with its bars and the chord of each one in a colour.

  3. 3

    Practise in parts

    Loop the hard section, set the speed from 25% to 150%, use the metronome and a count-in.

  • Chord per bar

    Each bar shows the detected chord in the colour of its note. You see the harmony before you play.

  • Synced tablature

    The cursor follows the sound and the chart keeps up. Click a bar to jump straight to it.

  • Loop any section

    Repeat just the chorus, or just that one bar, until it sounds clean.

  • Speed 25–150%

    Slow down without changing the pitch and speed up as you gain confidence.

  • Play along

    Mute your track and play with the band, or hear only your instrument.

  • Chart as PDF

    Edit chords and section names and export the map to take to rehearsal.

  • Metronome and count-in

    A count-in before you start and subdivisions (1 & 2 &) so you never lose the beat.

  • My songs

    The songs you import are saved in this browser and open where you left them.
