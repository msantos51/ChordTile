Terms of use

Mapa de Compassos is a tool for studying music: it reads a file you choose, shows the structure of the song by sections and bars, and plays the tab so you can practise.

  • The files are yours. Only load files you have the right to use, for example your own transcriptions, files you bought or files the author shares for study. Many songs and transcriptions are protected by copyright.
  • Files never leave your device. They are read and processed only in your browser, and stored there so you can open them again. They are not uploaded, stored on a server or shared with anyone, unless you sign in to sync “My songs” across your devices (see Privacy).
  • The chart is an estimate. The chord of each bar is detected automatically and may be wrong. You can fix any bar.
  • The tool is provided as is, without warranties. We are not responsible for how you use the files you load.

Privacy

  • We don’t collect personal data. There are no accounts, forms, advertising cookies or visitor statistics.
  • What is stored in your browser: the songs you load (in “My songs”, so you don’t have to load them again), your edits to each song (chords, section names and splits, chosen instrument), your preferences (bars per row, theme, sync) and whether you have seen the tour. Everything stays in the browser’s local storage (IndexedDB and localStorage), only on your device, and nothing is sent to a server. You can delete each song in “My songs”, or everything by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • If you sign in (optional): to keep “My songs” on all your devices, we store your email address, the files of the songs you load and their details (title, artist, number of bars, when you added and last opened them) in your account. They are hosted by Supabase, are private to you and are never shared. When you delete a song it is deleted from your account too. Signing out keeps the songs in that browser. To delete your account and everything in it, contact us.
  • No third-party requests: the fonts, the player and the sounds are served by the site itself (the only exception is Supabase, and only if you sign in). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any server, receives the IP address of visitors in order to deliver the pages; see the GitHub privacy statement.
