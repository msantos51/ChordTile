Questions

Good to know.

Which files can I open?

Guitar Pro 3 to 8 (.gp3, .gp4, .gp5, .gpx, .gp) and MusicXML. If a file opens in Guitar Pro but not here, save it as .gp5.

Do my files get uploaded?

No. Files are read in your browser. They only go to your account if you sign in and sync your songs across devices.

Does it work for bass?

Yes, guitar and bass. The chord of each bar is detected from the instrument you pick.

Can I cancel Premium?

Any time. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and you keep all your songs.
