The method in 4 moves

Why it works

Reading a tab isn’t knowing a song.

If your eyes are on the tab, the song lives on the screen and not in your hands. Tile by Tile hides the tab step by step, until it’s all in your head.

Small pieces

One section at a time, never the whole song at once. It’s split for you from the file.

Remember, don’t read

The tab hides and only the coloured tiles stay. You play it from memory, and it sticks.

Build the whole

Each new section is joined to the ones before, so the song grows in one piece.

See yourself get there

Every song shows how much you know by heart.

Your progress is saved to your account. Pick up where you stopped, on any device.
