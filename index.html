For guitar & bass players who struggle to memorise tabs

Can’t memorise tabs? Learn the song anyway.

ChordTile breaks any song into small sections: learn one with the tab, play it without, join it to the rest. Before you notice, you’re playing the whole song with no tab at all.

Try it on the sample

Works with your Guitar Pro (.gp, .gp5, .gpx) & MusicXML files

The Tile by Tile method, in 4 moves.

  1. e|---1-----|
B|-1---1---|
G|-------2-|
D|-3-------|
    01Learn it with the tabOne section on loop, as slow as you need.
  2. FGCAm
    02Play it withoutThe tab hides and only the chords stay. Three clean runs and it’s yours.
  3. Verse+Chorus
    03Join it upPlay it straight after the sections you already know.
  4. 04Play it all, no tabThe whole song, start to finish, no reading.

Sound familiar?

  • You can play the riff while the tab is open. Close it and it’s gone.
  • You know the intro of fifty songs and the whole of none.
  • You learn a song in a weekend and forget it by Friday.

You’re not bad at memorising. You’re trying to remember the whole tab at once. ChordTile gives you the song in pieces small enough to keep.
